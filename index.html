GETTING STARTED

A few things to know.

How do I start a trip?

Open the demo to enter a sample trip with five friends. In the planned connected app, a trip creator can invite friends, plan stops, pin announcements, and close the trip.

Can I install Trip Friend on my phone?

On Android, open the site in Chrome and use its install or home screen option when available. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. You can also use the browser directly.

Will calls work over Bluetooth or satellite?

The demo previews the walkie-talkie and incoming-call screens. Live group audio would use the internet. Bluetooth phone-to-phone voice and satellite communication are outside this web version’s capabilities.

Can I try reminders and custom sounds?

Yes. Try a repayment reminder in Expenses, or upload and preview a sound in Chat. The demo displays alerts locally. In a connected version, closed-app alerts would depend on browser and device settings, and uploaded sounds would play inside the open app.

Will my friends see what I enter?

Demo expenses, messages and photos stay in this browser. Sample map pins and call alerts illustrate how the connected app would work. The demo requests no GPS access and moves no money.
